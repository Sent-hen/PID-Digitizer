# Sample drawing

`dataset_pid_10.png` is sheet 10 of the Dataset-P&ID subset of PID2Graph, a **validation** sheet that is never used for training or testing. It has been converted to grayscale and is otherwise unmodified.

- **Dataset-P&ID:** Paliwal et al., "Digitize-PID: Automatic Digitization of Piping and Instrumentation Diagrams", PAKDD 2021.
- **PID2Graph:** Stürmer, Graumann & Koch, IEEE DSAA 2025 (https://zenodo.org/records/14803338).
- **License:** CC BY-SA 4.0.

`make demo` uploads it to the running service (`scripts/demo.py`).
